🌱 Greening Baner-Pashan Hills Since 2006

Transforming Barren Hills into Living Biodiversity

Over 10,000+ native trees planted across Tukai Hill, Baner Hill, and Ramnadi watershed with sustainable water harvesting and 100% community volunteers.

🤝 Daily Volunteer Drives

Join us every morning from 7:00 AM to 9:00 AM at Tukai Hill, Baner. No registration fees, equipment provided. Just bring a water bottle!

🌳 Native Trees Only

We nurture native biodiversity: Banyan (Vad), Peepal, Neem, Karanj, Gulmohar, and Cassia fistula, supporting local bird & insect life.

💳 80G Tax Exemption

All donations to Vasundhara Abhiyan are 50% tax-exempt under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act. 100% funds go directly to drip irrigation and saplings.
